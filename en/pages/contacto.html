FAQ

What kind of projects do you take on?

UX/UI design for websites and digital products, design and icon systems, digital campaign assets, visual identity, video editing and motion graphics.

How do we get started?

Write to me through the form or by email telling me what you need, what it's for and your timeline. With that, I'll get back to you to plan the next steps.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I live in Buenos Aires Province, Argentina, and I work remotely with clients anywhere in the world, in English or Spanish.

What tools do you use?

Figma for UX/UI; Photoshop, Illustrator and Lightroom for graphics; Premiere Pro and After Effects for video and motion; and HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React for the web.
