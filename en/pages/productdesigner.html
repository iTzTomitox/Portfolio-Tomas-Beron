What I do

  • I design complete websites from wireframe to production: I delivered 3 sites end to end, several of them led on my own.
  • I build mobile and desktop prototypes and validate responsive behavior before handoff to development.
  • I created 2 custom icon systems that are still in use across the company's and its clients' products.
  • I defined Google Business Profile design systems applied to 3 client accounts, unifying their visual identity.
  • I run design QA with the development team before every launch to ensure fidelity between the design and the live site.
  • I join client meetings with the PM and coordinate tasks with the design team.

Compra Gamer

As an academic project, I redesigned Compra Gamer, an e-commerce specialized in gaming hardware and accessories.

Methods applied

  • Usability testing to identify improvement points in the current experience.
  • Atomic Design to keep components consistent.
  • Interactive Figma prototypes to visualize the final proposal.

Outcome

The final design improves navigation, simplifies checkout and aims for higher user satisfaction.
